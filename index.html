Common questions

What cooperative leaders ask us.

What will KonnectCore cost?

Organisations selected for the onboarding programme are onboarded free — setup, records digitisation and training included. The first 20 organisations keep founding-member pricing, locked for life. You'll always know the price before you pay anything.

Does it work on basic phones and with poor internet?

Yes. KonnectCore is designed for the conditions cooperatives actually work in — it runs on any smartphone, and you can record entries even when connectivity is unreliable. No computer required.

Who owns our records? Is our members' data safe?

Your cooperative owns its data, full stop. Your records are stored securely, only your authorized officers can access them, and you can export everything at any time. We never share or sell your data.

Our members aren't tech-savvy. Can one officer manage everything?

That's exactly how it's designed to work. Members don't need the app at all — your secretary or manager records savings, loans and produce on their behalf, and each member's history stays accurate and instantly available.

Is training included?

Yes. We personally onboard every selected organisation — setup, data entry of your existing records, and hands-on training for your officers, on WhatsApp or in person where possible.

What happens after I request a demo?

We contact you within 1 working day for a short conversation about your organisation. If it's a good fit, we arrange your demo and plan your onboarding. There's no obligation at any point.
