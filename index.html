Sound familiar?

Paper records are costing your organisation.

Most farmer organisations still run on passbooks, notebooks and spreadsheets. It works — until it doesn't.

✕

Books get lost or damaged

One missing passbook or spoiled ledger can erase a member's entire savings history — and their trust in you.

⚖

Balance disputes erode trust

When a member's records don't match yours, every cedi becomes an argument — and members start holding back.

⏱

Reports take days to prepare

AGM reports, loan summaries and audit documents mean days of searching through books that should take minutes.

?

Nobody knows the full picture

Member records in one book, produce in another, loans in a spreadsheet — no single view of how the organisation is really doing.

⚠

Knowledge sits with one person

When the record-keeper travels, falls ill or steps down, the organisation's memory leaves with them.

✎

Errors multiply quietly

Hand-copied figures and manual totals drift over time — small mistakes become big discrepancies at audit.

Never lose a member's record again. KonnectCore keeps every member, savings, loan and produce record accurate, digital and audit-ready — so a lost notebook never becomes a lost history.

Common questions

What organisation leaders ask us.

What does KonnectCore cost?

Pricing depends on the size and needs of your organisation. Request a demo and we'll walk you through the platform, then agree a plan that fits — you'll always know the full cost before you pay anything.

Which devices does KonnectCore work on?

Administrators can use KonnectCore on smartphones, tablets and computers. It is designed for the conditions farmer organisations actually work in, including unreliable connectivity — no special equipment required.

Who owns our records? Is our members' data safe?

Your organisation owns its data, full stop. Records are stored securely, only your authorised officers can access them, and you can export everything at any time. We never share or sell your data.

Our members aren't tech-savvy. Can one officer manage everything?

Yes — that's exactly how it's designed to work. Members don't need the app at all. Your secretary, manager or field officers record contributions, loans and produce on their behalf, and each member's history stays accurate and instantly available.

We already have books and spreadsheets. Do we start from zero?

No. Guided onboarding includes record migration — we help you import your existing member, savings, loan and produce records so your history carries over into the platform.

Is training included?

Yes. Every organisation gets guided onboarding: platform setup, record migration and hands-on training for administrators and field officers — on WhatsApp, online or in person where possible.

What happens after I request a demo?

We contact you within 1 working day for a short conversation about your organisation, then arrange your demo and plan your onboarding together.
